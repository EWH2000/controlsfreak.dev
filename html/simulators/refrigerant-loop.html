The two gauges read like a manifold set: the low-side needle follows suction pressure, the high-side needle follows head. Each dial's inner scale is that refrigerant's own saturation-temperature ring — the P-T curve, on the gauge face. Superheat is referenced to the dew curve, subcooling to the bubble curve. An airside-starved coil can freeze with normal superheat — the refrigerant side looks fine while the coil ices, which is exactly why the freeze alarm fires on its own.
