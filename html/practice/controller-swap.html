Controller Swap Drill Field Drill

Ten questions on the real-world job of replacing a DDC controller — both halves of it. The hardware: documenting and re-landing field wiring, re-using the MS/TP station address and the BACnet device instance, and getting end-of-line termination right. The software: backing up the old config before it's gone, re-downloading the application, re-binding graphics, and actually commissioning the sequence instead of stopping at "the points read." Reveal panels explain inline; a few link out to the protocol and balancing lessons where the underlying concept lives.
