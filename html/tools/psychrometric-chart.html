Chart

Heavy curve = saturation (100% RH, the left/top boundary). Light curves rising to the right = constant relative humidity; light dashed lines sloping down to the right = constant wet-bulb (≈ constant enthalpy). States outside the drawn region still read out — they just aren't drawn.

Properties are per pound of dry air, from the ASHRAE formulations in US customary units; relative humidity is taken as the partial-pressure ratio (the usual approximation). Humidity ratio shown in grains per pound (7000 gr = 1 lb). Wet-bulb here is the thermodynamic wet-bulb, which a sling psychrometer reads to within a fraction of a degree. The drawn chart covers the lower-left region most HVAC air states live in; a state outside that range still reads out, it just isn't drawn.
