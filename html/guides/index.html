Guides

A field guide gathers everything on one subject — every tool, lesson, and drill — onto a single page, in a sensible order. Instead of hunting the calculators under Tools and the explainers under Education, you open one guide and the whole cluster is in front of you: what to read first, and what to reach for once you have a device or a duct in front of you.

Four guides so far, each a start-here reading path plus the tools that go with it. More as the clusters fill out.
