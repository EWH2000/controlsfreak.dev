About

Hi, I'm a controls programmer based in the Northeast U.S. This is a side project I'm working on while exploring vibe coding. There's a genuine gap in easy reference websites and educational resources for this trade. This site started as just a way to have some easy reference tools in one place, but has expanded to include much more.

All anecdotes on this site are real things I've encountered, not AI generated. Although it may refine my writing for some of them.

Bug reports, tool requests, or feedback — get in touch.

Education Sample

PID is three knobs that, together, push a process variable (PV — the thing you're measuring) toward a setpoint (SP — the value you want it to hold). This is a common sense walk through what each one does on a typical HVAC loop, plus three small sims you can play with one knob at a time.
