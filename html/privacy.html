Privacy

Privacy policy Privacy

Last updated: 2026-06-01

controlsfreak.dev is run by one person. There are no analytics, no ads, no user accounts, and nothing on the page that tries to identify you. The contact form is the only place the site asks for any information. This page covers what happens with that information, and what the few third-party services involved in serving the page can see.

Contact form

The contact form takes a name (optional), an email address (required), and a message. A Cloudflare Worker receives the submission at /api/contact and hands it to Resend, which delivers it to my inbox as an email.

That message is read only by me. It is not added to any mailing list, not shared with anyone else, and not used for anything other than replying. Resend and my email provider keep the message in their systems for as long as a delivered email normally lives there. The chain will be removed from my inbox once any discussion has concluded.

Cloudflare

The site sits behind Cloudflare, which serves the pages, blocks abusive traffic, and absorbs attacks. To do that, Cloudflare processes the standard request data your browser sends — including your IP address, the URL you requested, your User-Agent string, and similar headers. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for what they retain and for how long.

Cloudflare Turnstile

The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to keep bots out. Turnstile loads a small script from challenges.cloudflare.com and runs a silent check in your browser before the form will submit — there is no puzzle to solve. It shares minimal browser data with Cloudflare to evaluate that check.

Google Fonts

The page loads typography from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. As a side effect of those font requests, Google sees the IP address that requested them. See Google's privacy policy for what they do with it.

No analytics, no tracking, no cookies set by me

There is no analytics platform on this site, no advertising script, no fingerprinting, and no cookie set by the site itself. Nothing on the page tries to identify you across visits.

The site does keep a few small preference and progress values in your browser's localStorage so a page can remember your choices between visits: whether you have the US or Metric units toggle selected; the temperature range you picked on the psychrometric chart; and, on the practice quizzes, your best score, how many times you have attempted each one, and when you last played it.

All of that stays on your device. It is never sent to me or to anyone else, and it is not used to identify you or track you across visits. Clearing your browser's site data for controlsfreak.dev removes it.

Cloudflare may set its own cookies for security and bot protection, independently of anything on this site. Those are covered by their privacy policy linked above.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or the UK, the GDPR (or the UK GDPR) gives you a few rights over data that identifies you. In the case of this site, the data that identifies you is whatever you put into the contact form. You can ask me to send you a copy of what you submitted, correct it, or delete it. To make a request, send a note through the contact form from the same email address you used originally so I can match it up. I will reply as soon as possible. With the current number of emails, that normally means within 24 to 48 hours.

Request data that Cloudflare and Google see as part of delivering the page is handled under their own policies; if you want to exercise rights against them, you would need to contact them directly.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If something material changes — a new third-party service appears on a page, the contact form starts collecting something new — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date at the top will move.

Questions

For any privacy-related question, send a note through the contact form.
