Forced Air

Forced air is the side of the building that moves conditioned air. An air handler pulls in return air, mixes in outdoor air, drives it through filters and coils, and a fan pushes it out through ductwork to the zones. Every station on that path is something a control system measures, sizes, or holds at setpoint — mixed-air temperature, duct static pressure, the outdoor-air fraction, coil capacity, fan speed. These pages walk the air side end to end, from the air handler out to the VAV box, and give you the tools that put a number on each station.

New to it? Work the six lessons in order — they follow the air from the air handler out to the terminal boxes. Already have a unit in front of you? Jump to the tools: size a duct, average a traverse, work the minimum outdoor air, or check a coil. Everything here is open, needs no login, and tracks nothing.
