Education

Practical lessons for techs new to the industry. All of these are based on things I struggled with when I was new. This includes how a system actually works, why it's piped or wired the way it is, and what the equipment is doing. Not calculators (those live in Tools). If you have any requests or corrections, feel free to reach out.
